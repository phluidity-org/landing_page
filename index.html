The Problem

Valuations have grown.
Realized gains have not.

Companies are staying private longer than ever, and a canyon has opened between what shares are worth on paper and what holders can actually realize.

Secondary sales have become the market's release valve: vital, and woefully inadequate. Trades are opaque, episodic, expensive, and slow. For all but a handful of names, liquidity is effectively zero.

Introducing Phluidity

Phluidity: The Marketplace
for Private Companies.

Liquidity is the oxygen of markets. Without it, trades are infrequent, spreads are wide, and prices stay opaque. Phluidity uses tokenization to unblock the flow of private transactions, cutting costs, improving trust, and ultimately raising valuations.

One venue to issue, sell, buy, borrow, and lend, with verified counterparties and immutable ownership provenance.

The Solution

The same trade,
with Phluidity.

A liquid, accessible marketplace to buy, sell, or borrow against tokenized ownership interests. The same trade, opened to a wider universe of parties, with every friction removed.
